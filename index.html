About IndoCactus

IndoCactus is a listings site for Indonesian events in the Southwest United States and on the West Coast. It is based in Las Vegas, Nevada.

It covers festivals, concerts, film screenings, dance and gamelan performances, fashion, food events and community gatherings with a clear Indonesian connection — an Indonesian performer, organizer, community group, or a confirmed Indonesian programme inside a larger festival.

Coverage: Las Vegas (priority) · Los Angeles · San Diego · Orange County · Phoenix · Tucson · Albuquerque · Salt Lake City · Denver · San Francisco Bay Area · Sacramento · Portland · Seattle.

How a listing gets on: it needs a source a reader can open — an organizer page, venue or ticket listing, consulate or community post. The plan for hosting is GitHub Pages pointed at indocactus.com, at no cost beyond the domain David already bought ($11.81/year, expires November 21, 2027).
